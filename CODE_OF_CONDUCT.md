# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making SWE Index a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
community for everyone who wants to learn, contribute, and share opportunities.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intentions and discuss disagreements professionally.
- Provide useful and evidence-based feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Help maintain an environment where people feel comfortable contributing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Spam or repeated promotional content.
- Malicious contributions.
- Publishing private or sensitive information about others.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, close, or reject contributions and
conversations that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restrictions on participation
in the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the
project maintainer through the contact information associated with this
repository.

Please do not use public GitHub issues to report sensitive incidents.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the SWE Index repository,
including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related
spaces.
